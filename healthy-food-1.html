Exercises

Type your answers, then press Check. Wrong answers are marked in red: try again. The solution of a sentence is shown only after you have tried it at least twice.

1. Key words

Write the English word from the text (in orange) that corresponds to each Italian word.

2. Food words

Write the food word that matches each definition.

3. Complete the sentences

Complete each sentence about the text with one word. The first letter is given.

4. Say it in another way

Rewrite each sentence starting with the words given, without changing its meaning.

5. Questions on the text

Answer the questions with complete sentences, then compare your answer with the model.

6. My plate

Describe your usual lunch or dinner. Is it like the healthy plate? What can you change? (50–70 words) Then compare with the model.
